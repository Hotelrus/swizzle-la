The Archive

Every historical object we reproduce, and the reason we are entitled to reproduce it.

The Journal runs on real documents: menus, newspaper pages, photographs held in institutional collections. Showing them properly means knowing what each one is, who holds it, and where its copyright stands. These records set that out in full, including the searches that were run and the limits of what they prove. They are working documents rather than legal advice, and we would rather publish the reasoning than ask you to take the caption on trust.
