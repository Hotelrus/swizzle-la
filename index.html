Our Story

A meeting of islands & rivers

Swizzle is where the spirit of the Pacific meets the soul of Laos. By day this house is Noy's Fruit Heaven, pouring Vientiane's freshest shakes. At five, the totems wake: 65 cocktails built on aged rum, lao-lao and morning-market fruit, served under carvings that crossed the sea from Bali.

And when the bronze gong sounds, the whole room knows: a bowl is coming.

The Keepers of the House

Every tiki bar needs its keepers. Ours came a long way to light these torches.

Noy

Queen of the Fruit · Keeper of the Day

Noy has spent twenty years among mangoes, coconuts and morning markets, and she runs Noy's Fruit Heaven the way a captain runs a ship: nothing less than perfectly ripe gets aboard. Every syrup, puree and fruit in your glass tonight passed her hands first.

Vientiane 20 Years of Fruit Market at Dawn Fruit Heaven

Counted so far: twenty years, ten thousand mangoes, zero bad ones served. Well, maybe a few. Not on purpose.

Ruslan

The Navigator · Keeper of the Night

Twenty-three years in hospitality, collected the long way around. He started at seventeen in Russia, went to sea on a ship that ran from the Bahamas to Antarctica, and worked the bars and floors of Moscow, Singapore, Dubai and Thailand before the Mekong finally got him. He ran food and beverage for hotels for years. Now he lights the torches here.
